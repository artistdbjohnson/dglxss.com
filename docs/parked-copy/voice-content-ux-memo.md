# Voice / content UX — NetJets + EJM restyles

**Ask:** How we present *their* words better without inventing.  
**Scope:** Live Salesforce Experience Cloud (NetJets + EJM) vs Motionsites restyle.  
**Rule:** Exact copy. Quieter hierarchy. Not louder marketing.

---

## What live does wrong (content UX, not brand)

**Walls of text.** Homepage body dumps long paragraphs into undifferentiated blocks (NetJets pinnacle/body; EJM safety + who-we-are sit in the same visual weight as everything else). The eye never lands.

**Buried CTAs.** Primary asks hide in JS panels, mega-menus, or late bands. NetJets: `REQUEST INFORMATION` / `CALL US` are non-anchors until a panel opens; Hero on early rebuild even pointed at a dead `#request`. EJM: conversion lives mid-scroll (`Try it now >`, `Explore Our Network`) with no persistent quiet path after the video.

**Same shout level everywhere.** H1, H2, card titles, legal, and footer all compete. Experience Cloud chrome (utility bars, cookie walls, duplicate DOM) adds noise on top of the words.

**Nav as brochure.** Mega-menus carry the IA; the page itself doesn’t sequence a read. Words are inventory, not a path.

---

## What the restyle does (same words)

| Live habit | Restyle move |
|---|---|
| Text on hero media | **Hero = media only** (EJM: 3-clip video). Words start *under* — they get air. |
| One density for all copy | **Hierarchy by type/spacing/weight only** — not new adjectives. H1 breathes; body stays light; CTAs are few and obvious. |
| CTA buried / JS-only | **One clear primary + one secondary**, real hrefs (`/request`, `/s/contact-us`, live exact labels). No invented “Get a quote today.” |
| Long block as a slab | **Keep the paragraph; change the measure** — max-width, leading, section breaks. Same sentence, readable. |
| Mega-menu does the selling | **Scroll order locked to recon** — their sections, their sequence; chrome stays quiet. |

---

## Voice stance

We don’t rewrite NetJets or EJM into “studio voice.”  
We **stage** their voice: exact strings, trailing `>` where they use it, legal lines intact, phone and address exact.

Minimal ≠ empty. Minimal = **their line is the loudest thing in the band.**

If a line feels weak on the restyle, the fix is layout (size, contrast, isolation) — **not** a better line we made up.
